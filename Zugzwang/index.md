# Privacy Policy for Zugzwang

**Last updated:** September 16, 2026

Zugzwang is an alarm clock app developed for Android. This
policy explains what data the app handles and, just as importantly, what
it does not.

## Summary

Zugzwang does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. It has no
internet access, no analytics, no ad networks, and no account system.
Everything the app stores stays on your device, in a local database, and is
only accessible to the app itself.

## Data Stored on Your Device

To function, Zugzwang stores the following information locally on your
device only:

- **Alarms** — the times, repeat schedules, and settings you configure
- **Puzzle solve history** — a log of chess puzzles you've attempted, so the
  app can track your stats and streaks
- **Puzzle progress** — which puzzles you've already solved, so the app can
  avoid repeating them

None of this data ever leaves your device. The app has no server, makes no
network requests, and cannot transmit this data anywhere.

## Permissions Used

Zugzwang requests the following Android permissions, all of which are
required for it to work as an alarm clock:

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| Exact alarms (`USE_EXACT_ALARM`, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`) | To ring your alarm at the exact time you set |
| Full-screen intent | To show the puzzle screen over the lock screen when the alarm fires |
| Display over other apps | To reliably bring the puzzle to the foreground when the alarm rings |
| Wake lock / turn screen on | To wake your device's screen so you can see and solve the puzzle |
| Notifications | To show the alarm notification |
| Vibrate | To vibrate your device when the alarm rings |
| Foreground service (media playback) | To keep the alarm sound and screen active while ringing |
| Modify audio settings | To control alarm volume |
| Read media audio | To let you pick a custom alarm sound from your device |
| Boot completed | To re-schedule your alarms after your device restarts |
| Ignore battery optimizations | To prevent the OS from delaying or killing the alarm |

None of these permissions are used to collect or share personal data — they
exist solely to make sure your alarm reliably rings and wakes your device.

## Third-Party Services

Zugzwang does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking
services. It does not integrate with Firebase, Google Analytics, or any
similar service.

## Children's Privacy

Zugzwang does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone,
including children, because it does not collect personal information at
all.
